## Five-Pillar Review

Assess every pillar; do not treat the architect's self-assessment as independent
evidence. Rate the design against scoped criteria, not as WAF certification.

## Findings and Planning Inputs

Specific affected design/resource/module, impact, evidence, priority, owner,
required fix/decision and acceptance/retest. Preserve security/compliance finding
IDs and dispositions; do not overrule them or invent human approval. Record sources,
versions/access dates and questions where requirements cannot be assessed.

## Disposition and Limits

`PASS`, `FAIL` or `BLOCKED` for the reviewed design scope. Required missing criteria
block release; operational tests remain pending at their declared gate. No
percentage health score or legal/security certification is inferred from this review.
Changes to the design require affected findings/pillars to be re-reviewed.
