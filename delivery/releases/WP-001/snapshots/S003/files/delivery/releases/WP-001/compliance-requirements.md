## Applicability Boundary

One **non-production, empty foundation**, PUBLIC/SYNTHETIC business data only,
maximum three users, private-network-only application audience; accepted target,
dedicated POC groups, no-reuse direction and confirmed owner are not reopened.
“Empty” does not mean an empty subscription, anonymous administrators or absent
platform records. Entra identifiers, IPs, operator activity, alert contacts, support
records and telemetry can be personal data even without claimant workloads.

No numeric budget is known. Offer metadata, Enabled state and `spendingLimit=On`
are observations, not usable credit, commercial eligibility, contract acceptance
or spending approval. Regulated review depth does not make the POC a paying
agency, certify an AI system, or authorise regulated production data.

No added service is implicitly required/provisioned by this matrix. Foundry,
AI Search, Azure ML, application stores, evidence/messaging, applications,
claimant ingestion, remote model calls, acquisition/training/scoring/promotion
and real entitlement/payment integrations remain later packages. Define future
interfaces only; any changed data, geography, identity, exposure, source/licence
or model purpose triggers impact assessment and scoped approval.
