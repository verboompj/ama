# CAP Strategic Plan — Hungary

## What it is
"Magyarország KAP stratégiai terve, 2023–2027" is Hungary's national implementation plan for the Common Agricultural Policy, published and managed by the Hungarian Ministry of Agriculture (Agrárminisztérium) and its designated National Managing Authority.

## Coverage
- **Subsidy schemes**: funding measures, eligibility rules, and subsidy conditions for agriculture and rural development, including eco-schemes, investment aid, and rural innovation support.
- **Objectives**: income support combined with incentives for environmental sustainability — water, soil, biodiversity, and climate action.
- **Intervention logic**: justification linking the types of aid to EU objectives and Hungary's national priorities.
- **Budgets and allocations**: breakdown of CAP funds per pillar, sector, and scheme (direct payments, rural development, eco-schemes).
- **Application procedures**: calls for proposals, application guides and deadlines, managed and announced by the National Managing Authority.
