# Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) — Base Information

## What it is
The Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) is the European Union's main framework for supporting agriculture, rural development, and sustainable food production. It is managed by the European Commission's Directorate-General for Agriculture and Rural Development (DG AGRI).

## Aims
- Support farmers and improve agricultural productivity.
- Ensure a stable supply of affordable food.
- Safeguard a decent standard of living for farmers.
- Tackle climate change and ensure sustainable management of natural resources.
- Maintain vibrant rural areas and promote jobs in farming and related industries.

## Structure (2023–2027 period)
Funded at EU level from the EU budget, CAP consists of three main support types:
1. **Direct payments** — income support and environmental remuneration for farmers.
2. **Market measures** — tools to stabilize agricultural markets and manage crises (oversupply, demand shocks).
3. **Rural development** — national/regional programmes for rural infrastructure, young farmers, etc.

## Financials (2021–2027)
- Total CAP budget: **€386.6 billion**
  - **€291.1 billion** — European Agricultural Guarantee Fund (EAGF, "Pillar 1" — direct support).
  - **€95.5 billion** — European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development (EAFRD, "Pillar 2").

## Legal Basis
- Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (Articles 38–44).
- Regulation (EU) 2021/2115 (CAP Strategic Plans).
- Regulation (EU) 2021/2116 (financing, management, monitoring).
- Regulation (EU) 2021/2117 (common market organisation amendments).

## Key Requirements for Farmers
- Compliance with environmental, animal welfare, and food safety standards ("enhanced conditionality") to receive direct payments.
- Each Member State implements a national CAP Strategic Plan aligned with EU objectives.
- Each Member State publishes CAP subsidy recipient details per EU transparency rules.
