# Hungary Agriculture Administration

## Paying Agency
Hungary's accredited CAP paying agency is the **Magyar Államkincstár (Hungarian State Treasury)**, operating as Hungary's national paying agency for EU agricultural funds. It is the sole accredited body responsible for receiving, assessing, and disbursing CAP payments, covering both:
- **EAGF** — European Agricultural Guarantee Fund (direct payments/Pillar 1)
- **EAFRD** — European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development (Pillar 2)

This is real-world institutional research. The Field & File organisation and personas
are fictional; the case's payment volumes and control gaps are scenario assumptions,
not asserted facts about MÁK.

## Responsibilities
- Manages payments under strict EU rules and is directly accountable for the legality and correctness of the payment process.
- Distributes funds to a wide range of beneficiaries, from small family farms to large agricultural cooperatives.
- Runs digital tools for farmers, including the **mobilGAZDA** app for tracking payment status and submitting documentation.
- Handles hundreds of subsidy schemes tied to the CAP Strategic Plan 2023–2027 (agri-environmental, animal welfare, youth, modernization, training, forestry, resilience measures).
- Publishes calls for applications, rule changes, and beneficiary communications via the national CAP portal and its own platforms.

## Governance Context
- Ministerial oversight from the **Ministry of Agriculture (Agrárminisztérium)**.
- Operates under EU Commission audit interfaces and reporting obligations (see `regulatory-compliance/` for the applicable EU frameworks).
