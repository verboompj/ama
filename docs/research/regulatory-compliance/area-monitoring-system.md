# Regulatory Compliance: Area Monitoring System (AMS) Requirements

## Standard
The Area Monitoring System (AMS) requirement stems from:
- **Article 70(1), Regulation (EU) 2022/2116** — AMS mandatory use for area-based CAP schemes from 1 January 2023, full operational status from 1 January 2024.
- **Article 10(4), Regulation (EU) 2022/1173** — from 1 January 2025, any eligibility condition monitorable via Copernicus Sentinel data (or geolocated photos) is deemed "monitorable" under CAP rules.

## Scope
AMS is defined as the regular and systematic observation, tracking, and assessment of agricultural activities using Copernicus Sentinel satellite data (or data of at least equivalent value).
- Relies primarily on **Sentinel-1** (radar) and **Sentinel-2** (optical) imagery, refreshed every 3–5 days.
- Enables automated analysis (mowing, ploughing, crop cover change detection) to reduce reliance on manual on-the-spot checks.
- Feeds directly into the **Integrated Administration and Control System (IACS)** and the **Land Parcel Identification System (LPIS)**.
- Covers schemes such as Basic Income Support for Sustainability (BISS), Areas of Natural Constraint (ANC), Protein Aid Scheme (PAS), Straw Incorporation Measure (SIM), and eco-schemes.
- Member States must annually assess system quality using an EU-level methodology.

## Relevance to Case Study 47 (Hungarian Paying Agency)
This is the core driver of the transformation objective: the agency "cannot review every parcel every season" under the old sample-based model, and full-coverage AMS-based verification (via a Parcel Monitoring Agent) is mandated and cheaper than continuing to incur Commission financial corrections.
