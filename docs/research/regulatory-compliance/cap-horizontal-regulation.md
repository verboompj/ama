# Regulatory Compliance: CAP Horizontal Regulation (Financing, Management & Monitoring)

## Standard
**Regulation (EU) 2021/2116** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 2 December 2021 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy, repealing Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013. Commonly called the "CAP Horizontal Regulation."

## Scope
- Companion regulation to the CAP Strategic Plan Regulation (2021/2115); governs how CAP expenditure is financed, managed, and controlled rather than what is funded.
- Establishes Member State management and control system obligations, including the Integrated Administration and Control System (IACS) and the Land Parcel Identification System (LPIS).
- Sets out clearance-of-accounts and conformity-clearance procedures — the legal basis for the Commission financial corrections referenced in the case study.
- Shifts the CAP delivery model toward greater Member State subsidiarity, performance-based reporting, and administrative simplification (2023–2027 period).
- Retains EAGF and EAFRD as the financing funds.

## Relevance to Case Study 47 (Hungarian Paying Agency)
This is the direct legal basis for the paying agency's obligation to run IACS/LPIS-based checks, and for the €60 million in Commission financial corrections at stake. The Audit Evidence Agent's "Commission audit packs" exist to satisfy this Regulation's clearance/conformity procedures.
