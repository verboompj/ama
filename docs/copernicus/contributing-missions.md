# Copernicus Contributing Missions (CCM)

Commercial and public Earth observation satellite missions that complement the core Sentinel satellites, providing additional high-resolution, very-high-resolution (VHR), and specialized data types. Operated by ESA, its Member States, EUMETSAT, and third-party (including commercial "New Space") operators.

## Categories
- Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)
- Optical sensors
- Altimetry systems
- Radiometers
- Spectrometers
- Emerging domains: hyperspectral, thermal infrared imaging

## Example Missions
- **ENVISAT** (incl. MERIS instrument)
- **SMOS** (Soil Moisture and Ocean Salinity)
- **Landsat 5, 7, 8, 9**
- **PRISMA** (Italy)
- **CryoSat** (ESA)
- **SUOMI NPP** (USA)
- Various European "New Space" and commercial VHR constellation operators

## Scale
The CCM portfolio includes 150+ missions from roughly 25 European and international entities. In early 2025, ESA and the European Commission signed agreements with 19 additional satellite data companies to expand CCM data streams. The list is continuously evolving.
