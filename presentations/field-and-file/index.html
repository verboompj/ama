10 / Lessons for the next delivery stages

Keep customer outcomes at the centre.

Heavy assurance requirements created competing agent interpretations and increased the sponsor’s coordination load.

Implementation goal

Validate a focused technical PoC.

Assurance demands

Competing approval interpretations slowed handoffs between agents.

The human referee

Set the priority.
Resolved agent conflicts.
Clarified scope and ownership.

User’s retrospective
What helped

Explicit ownership, visible blockers, recorded decisions and traceable artifacts.

What cost time

Heavyweight standards, recursive coordination and a complex test evaluator.

1 / Agree success early

Define customer outcomes, the experiment scope and acceptance criteria together.

2 / Keep checks practical

Use focused deterministic checks and proven tools for each delivery stage.

3 / Assign conflict ownership

Give each disagreement one accountable owner and a clear decision path.

Learning: align agents around customer value, proportionate assurance and one accountable decision path.
Lessons from the project; proposed improvements for the next delivery stages.
