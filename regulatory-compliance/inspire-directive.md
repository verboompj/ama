# Regulatory Compliance: INSPIRE Directive

## Standard
**Directive 2007/2/EC** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 March 2007 establishing an Infrastructure for Spatial Information in the European Community (INSPIRE).

## Scope
- Sets general rules for creating a spatial data infrastructure in Europe, primarily to support EU environmental policy and related activities.
- Applies to spatial data held by public authorities, in electronic format, covering areas under Member State jurisdiction.
- Requires harmonisation and interoperability of spatial data infrastructures across borders via common Implementing Rules on metadata, data specifications, network services, and data/service sharing.
- Defines core concepts: spatial data, spatial data sets, spatial data services, spatial objects, metadata, interoperability, and the INSPIRE geo-portal.

## Relevance to Case Study 47 (Hungarian Paying Agency)
The Land Parcel Identification System (LPIS) — the geospatial reference database underlying the Parcel Monitoring Agent's per-parcel classification — is a spatial data set subject to INSPIRE's interoperability, metadata, and publication requirements, ensuring Hungary's parcel data is structured and shareable in a way compatible with EU-wide monitoring and audit needs.
