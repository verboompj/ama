# Regulatory Compliance: CAP Strategic Plan Regulation

## Standard
**Regulation (EU) 2021/2115** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 2 December 2021, establishing rules on support for strategic plans to be drawn up by Member States under the Common Agricultural Policy (CAP Strategic Plans), financed by the EAGF and EAFRD, and repealing Regulations (EU) No 1305/2013 and (EU) No 1307/2013.

## Scope
Sets out the framework for the design and implementation of CAP Strategic Plans by Member States for the 2023–2027 period, including:
- Common EU objectives and indicators.
- Types of interventions Member States may fund (direct payments, sectoral interventions, rural development).
- Requirements for Member State strategic plans (needs assessment, intervention strategy, governance).
- Financial arrangements supported by EAGF and EAFRD.

## Relevance to Case Study 47 (Hungarian Paying Agency)
Hungary's national CAP Strategic Plan (2023–2027) must comply with this Regulation. It defines the eligibility conditions, eco-schemes, and financial rules the paying agency (Magyar Államkincstár) must administer and verify for the ~160,000 farmers and €2 billion/year in subsidies.
