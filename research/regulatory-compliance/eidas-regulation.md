# Regulatory Compliance: eIDAS Regulation

## Standard
**Regulation (EU) No 910/2014** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 2014 on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market and repealing Directive 1999/93/EC (eIDAS Regulation).

## Scope
- Establishes a legal framework for electronic identification (eID) of natural and legal persons across the EU.
- Governs trust services: electronic signatures, electronic seals, time stamps, electronic delivery services.
- Requires mutual recognition of notified eID schemes between Member States.

## Relevance to Case Study 47 (Hungarian Paying Agency)
Farmers authenticate and submit claims, evidence, and disputes through the national portal referenced in the case study (where the Farmer Guidance and Discrepancy Resolution Agents operate). eIDAS governs the legal validity of that electronic identification and any electronic signatures/submissions (e.g., accepted evidence, claim confirmations) used in the claims and discrepancy-resolution workflow.
