# Regulatory Compliance: Web Accessibility Directive

## Standard
**Directive (EU) 2016/2102** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 October 2016 on the accessibility of the websites and mobile applications of public sector bodies.

## Scope
- Sets harmonised accessibility requirements for websites and mobile apps of public sector bodies across the EU, particularly for people with disabilities.
- Requires accessibility statements, monitoring, and reporting obligations by Member States.

## Relevance to Case Study 47 (Hungarian Paying Agency)
The national portal used by the Farmer Guidance Agent and Discrepancy Resolution Agent to interact with 160,000 farmers is a public-sector digital service, so it must meet these accessibility standards — ensuring farmers with disabilities can access eligibility guidance, submit geo-tagged evidence, and respond to discrepancy notices before payment.
