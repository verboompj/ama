# Regulatory Compliance: GDPR

## Standard
**Regulation (EU) 2016/679** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation).

## Scope
- Governs the processing of personal data of individuals within the EU, including lawful basis, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity/confidentiality, and accountability.
- Grants data subjects rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection.
- Requires data protection by design and by default, and impact assessments (DPIAs) for high-risk processing.

## Relevance to Case Study 47 (Hungarian Paying Agency)
Farmer-facing agents (Farmer Guidance Agent, Discrepancy Resolution Agent) process personal and farm data (identity, geo-tagged photos, claims). GDPR governs:
- Lawful processing of farmer personal data collected via the national portal.
- Farmers' administrative-law right to be heard alongside GDPR data-subject rights when discrepancies or automated classifications affect them.
- Data minimisation and security requirements for audit evidence packs shared with the EU Commission.
